How it works

Three steps. About two minutes.

No software installations, no logins. Just pure browser-native streaming.

1

Add your movie

Drop any video file directly into your browser. Cineva prepares the stream locally and provides a shareable party key.

2

Send the link

Copy your unique 6-character room code or instant link. Friends open it on desktop, tablet, or VR headset.

3

Press play

The host starts playback. Every client syncs to within 20 milliseconds, leaving you to enjoy movie night.
